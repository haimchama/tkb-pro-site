More than messages

Everything you already do in a chat, plus the things you usually need three other apps for.

  • Chats and groups

    Text, photos, voice notes, polls and stickers, one-to-one or in groups. Your own chat with yourself is always pinned at the top for notes.

  • Tasks and checklists

    Send a task or a shopping list right inside the chat. Everyone can tick items off, add more, and see what is still left.

  • Reminders for others

    Remind your partner about the barber at 17:30, with an alert an hour before. They get it on time and you see when they confirm.

  • Walkie-talkie

    Hold the mic and talk; it plays on their phone right away. Block it per contact, or allow it only during work hours.

  • Read receipts your way

    Turn read receipts on or off for each person separately, not just for everyone at once.

  • A private password vault

    Keep your passwords in a vault that is encrypted on your phone and opens with your master password or fingerprint.
