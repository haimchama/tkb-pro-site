What's inside

In Hebrew, right to left, made for phones.

  • Real-time chats

    One-to-one and group chats with sent, delivered and read marks, photos, videos, files and locations.

  • Voice notes and a walkie-talkie

    Record a voice note and read it as text, or hold the talk button and speak straight to a contact.

  • Calendar, tasks and reminders

    A calendar with Hebrew dates, tasks you can tick off inside a chat, and reminders that pop up on time.

  • Your text messages too

    On Android, the phone's SMS conversations can sit in the same list as your chats. They stay on the phone.

  • A password vault

    Passwords encrypted with a master password of your own, unlocked with your fingerprint.

  • Make it yours

    Themes from luxury to alien, dark or light, and a full demo with sample data to look around first.
