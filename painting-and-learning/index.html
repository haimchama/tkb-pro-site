Paint & Learn

Paint, play and learn

Paint & Learn is free and made for small hands.

  • Nine coloring games

    Coloring puzzles, a free painting studio, mandalas, neon drawing, pixel art, scratch cards, an aquarium and stickers.

  • Learning along the way

    Shapes, letters and numbers appear inside the games and are read aloud.

  • A profile for each child

    Every child picks a character and collects stars and levels of their own.

  • New games arrive by themselves

    More learning and play games are added to the app without reinstalling it.

  • No ads

    No ads of any kind, no in-app purchases, and nothing that leads a child out of the app.

  • Private by design

    No accounts and no sign-in. Progress stays on the device.
