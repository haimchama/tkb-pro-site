A quiet moment, one object at a time

Zen Room is free to play and built to be gentle.

  • Tidy up, your way

    Drag objects from the tray into place and finish with up to three stars. In some levels the objects hide around the room, and you find them first.

  • 100 levels, 40 cozy rooms

    A path of hand-made rooms that unlocks as you go, from bedrooms to forest cabins. New levels arrive in the game by themselves.

  • Stars, coins and power-ups

    Earn coins and open gift boxes. Add time, freeze time, or let a power-up place one object for you.

  • Calm or challenging

    On Easy the time limit is optional: turn it off and take as long as you like. Want more? Medium, Hard and Epic tighten the clock.

  • No forced ads

    No pop-ups, no banners. Rewarded videos for bonuses play only when you choose to watch one.

  • One-time Ad-free purchase

    Prefer to skip videos? Buy Ad-free once and get the bonuses instantly.
