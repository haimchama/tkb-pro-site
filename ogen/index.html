Ogen עוגן

The platform for therapy clinics. The diary, patient files, video sessions and messages in one place, with a personal area and an app for every patient.

Coming soon toGoogle Play Request a demo

Everything a clinic runs on

Built in Hebrew, right to left, for phones first.

  • Diary and scheduling

    Works with the clinic's Google Calendar: every therapist's diary, availability, appointment reminders and attendance.

  • Patient files

    A file for every patient with intake forms, session summaries, history, documents to sign and a payments ledger with receipts.

  • Video sessions

    The therapist opens a call and the patient's phone rings. On Android the call fills the screen, even when the phone is locked.

  • A personal area for patients

    Appointments, messages, questionnaires and forms, in the clinic's own space. Notifications for reminders and new messages.

  • Games for patients

    Chess, checkers, memory, tic-tac-toe and four in a row: alone, against the computer, or with the therapist in a session. Points, levels, streaks and a daily challenge.

  • Private by design

    Each clinic has its own database. Access by role, no ads and no analytics. Notifications carry no names, times or content.
