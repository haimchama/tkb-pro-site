Garage Master

A customer describes a problem in their own words. You run the checks, decide what is broken, pick the right tool and fix the car. Along the way you learn how a car really works.

Coming soon toGoogle Play

Think like a mechanic

Garage Master is free, and nothing in it is a guess-the-button game.

  • Find the fault yourself

    Every check gives you a finding, never the answer. You read what you found and decide which part is to blame. A wrong call costs you the part.

  • Pick the tool, do the work

    Open the bonnet, drag the right tool onto the part, pull the old part out and fit the new one. Hands-on 3D repairs are being added job by job.

  • Learn something on every job

    Batteries, brakes, cooling, charging and more. Each finished job ends with one plain fact about the part you just replaced.

  • Grow your garage

    Start with a few hand tools. Pay buys better ones, finished jobs raise your garage level, and each level brings harder faults.

  • New jobs arrive by themselves

    More cars and faults are added to the game without reinstalling it. Plays in English and Hebrew.

  • No ads, private by design

    No ads, no accounts and no sign-in. Your garage stays on your device.
