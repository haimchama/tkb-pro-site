Yachdav Fit יחדיו פיט

A home workout coach for beginners. Short workouts with no equipment, a coach that adjusts to you, and reminders at the level you choose.

What's inside

In Hebrew, right to left, made for phones.

  • 11 well-known programs

    The 7-minute workout, 5BX, Tabata, the NHS strength and flex plan, the one-minute workout and more, each in a home version with its source.

  • A coach that adjusts to you

    Every exercise has "hard", "easy" and "it hurts". The next workout follows how the last one went. Nothing pushes you through pain.

  • Your week at a glance

    Rings for workouts, minutes and XP, levels and streaks. A rest day is a choice, not a broken streak.

  • Reminders with attitude

    Your coach reminds you to train, drink, move, sleep and weigh in, at four levels from easy-going to celebrity trainer.

  • Weight tracking

    Your current weight, your goal and how far you have come, with a chart and a weekly weigh-in.

  • A leaderboard, if you want one

    Join with a nickname and compare weekly XP. Weight and health data never leave the phone.
